Notification Settings

Configure how you receive alerts. WebSocket (in-app) is enabled by default.

Desktop (OS) Notifications

System popups (Windows Action Center, macOS Notification Center). Enable to get alerts when the app is in the background.

Checking...

Click Enable to allow desktop notifications. You can also enable by clicking the notification bell in the top bar.

Show OS popup notifications

When enabled, notifications appear as system popups. Turn off to stop OS popups (in-app notifications still work).

Channels

Enable notification channels. You must provide an email address when enabling email.

In-App (WebSocket)

Real-time notifications in the app. Enabled by default for all users.

Email

Receive notifications by email. Enter your email address below when enabled.

Mobile (FCM)

Push notifications to your mobile app. Register your device via the app when prompted.

Event Notifications (by Severity)

Choose channels for detection events: critical (fall, fire, weapon), warning, info.

System Notifications

Camera offline and report ready alerts.
